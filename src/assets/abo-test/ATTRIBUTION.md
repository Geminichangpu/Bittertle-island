Amazon Berkeley Objects 3D models

Original 3D models and data: Amazon.com, Amazon Berkeley Objects dataset.
Source: https://registry.opendata.aws/amazon-berkeley-objects/
Model files optimized and distributed by CodePawl: https://huggingface.co/datasets/codepawl/abo-glb
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: CodePawl re-encoded and resized textures and compressed geometry. This island adjusts model placement, scale and orientation; it adds procedural plants and a birdhouse support, and instances the beach loungers.
Brands and product names remain the property of their respective owners.

Wall textures: Plaster001 and Plaster002 by ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com/), and brown_planks_05 by Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/), CC0 1.0. The plaster color maps were converted to low-contrast neutral derivatives to retain the island's original colors; original OpenGL normal and roughness maps are used.
